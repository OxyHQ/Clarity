# Product feeds

> **Clarity is Oxy's one crawler of stores. It fetches and keeps the source
> documents; what they mean commercially is the consumer's reading.**

A product feed is a public shop catalogue Clarity polls: a Shopify store's
`/meta.json` and `/products.json`, or the Steam store's game search for one
market. Clarity reads it under robots.txt for ClarityBot (`Content-Signal:
search=no` and `Crawl-delay` honoured, the delay shared across every feed on an
origin), at the provider's own polite pause, and keeps each document exactly as
the source served it. It does not price, match or identify anything: Mercaria
pulls the documents through `@clarity.surf/sdk` and reads them with its own
mapping, so variants, prices and identity mean what Mercaria says.

## Where it lives

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| Kinds: what they request, how a body splits into documents (pure) | `packages/backend/src/search/products/feeds/providers.ts` |
| Registration, polling, pruning, record pages | `packages/backend/src/search/products/feeds/feeds.ts` |
| Tables | `clarity_product_feeds`, `clarity_product_feed_records` (`0037`) |
| Worker loop (its own, beside job feeds) | `runProductFeedLoop` in `src/worker.ts` |
| API (`clarity:index`) | `PUT /v1/product-feeds`, `GET /v1/product-feeds/:id`, `GET /v1/product-feeds/:id/records?cursor=&limit=` |
| SDK | `productFeeds.register / get / records` |

## Kinds

| Kind | Identifier | `meta` | One record | Bound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `shopify_storefront` | store domain | the store's `/meta.json` (must name a currency) | one `/products.json` product object, keyed by product id | 40 pages (10,000 products) a poll; a larger store finishes over the next polls |
| `steam_store` | market country (`ES`, `PT`, `FR`, `IT`, `DE`, `GB`, `US`) | `{ country, currency, language }` | `{ html }`, one search row, keyed by app id; bundles skipped | the first 10,000 games in Steam's relevance order |

## A poll is a walk; only a finished walk prunes

- Each document read is upserted with `lastSeenAt`; `changedAt` moves only when
  its content hash changes.
- A walk that reaches the end DELETES what it did not see since it began: the
  store stopped listing it, so Clarity stops serving it and the consumer's own
  freshness rule retires the offer.
- A walk cut short by its page budget resumes from its cursor five minutes
  later and prunes nothing until it finishes. `lastCompletedAt` says when the
  records last were a whole catalogue.
- **A fresh walk that finds nothing prunes nothing** — an empty answer from a
  store that listed products a moment ago is far likelier an outage than a
  closed shop. `lastError` says so.
- Feeds poll every six hours by default; a failing one backs off at least six.
  Due feeds are claimed under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`.

## Registering is idempotent

`PUT /v1/product-feeds { kind, identifier, label? }` answers the feed already
registered for that source, or registers it — in its canonical spelling
(`https://Shop.Example/` → `shop.example`). A consumer calls it before every
read; an operator's settings on an existing feed are never overwritten.
